About Me

Hello, my name is Brandon Mario Bahena. I recently graduated from the City Colleges of Chicago at Harold Washington College with an Associate of Science. I am currently attending NEIU (Northeastern Illinois University), majoring in Computer Science and pursuing a career as a Software Engineer. I have some experience with HTML and CSS, and I know Python and JavaScript — I'm looking forward to learning more about web development and other programming languages.

A Bio About my Journey

Growing up in Little Village, my education was never just about me—it represented resilience, overcoming setbacks, and proving that financial hardships wouldn't define my future. Initially, I wanted to become a police officer or FBI agent, but my mother strongly opposed it. Ironically, she was fine with me joining the army. Ultimately, my passion for technology led me toward Computer Science. Middle school was my turning point. My teacher, Mr. Pratt, introduced me to coding, and I was instantly captivated by how simple commands could create complex functions. In high school, I joined an after-school program where I repaired computers and programmed Arduino circuits, deepening my love for problem-solving. College further cemented my passion as I worked with data structures, algorithms, and abstract programming concepts, finally bringing my childhood curiosity full circle. Now, I aspire to become a Software Engineer, pushing the boundaries of innovation and problem-solving. NEIU's Computer Science program offers the resources, mentorship, and opportunities I need to turn that goal into reality. My journey—marked by challenges but fueled by determination—will serve as a testament to my community that, despite setbacks, perseverance can lead to success.
